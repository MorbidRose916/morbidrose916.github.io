2. A House Built to Erase

Finished: 2023 Published: March 22, 2026

Every disagreement
warped…
not into resolution,
but into something unrecognizable,
like a mirror that refused
to return me to myself.

I brought you truth
in open hands,
and you returned it altered…
twisted, thinned, and hollowed out,
until I no longer trusted
what I was holding…
or the hands holding it.

My voice existed there,
but only as something passing through.
It drifted through rooms
that devoured sound…
each word collapsing on itself
before it could take shape.

You rewrote the past
with quiet precision…
not violently,
but thoroughly...
until every memory I reached for
felt tampered with,
like something I was never meant to keep.

Out there,
you were warmth…
easy, bright, undeniable.

In here,
you were something heavier…
not force, not chaos…
but pressure...
slow, constant, inescapable...
until even my thoughts
felt like something I had to hide.

I tried to draw lines…
not to divide us,
but to preserve something of myself...
but you crossed them
without hesitation,
as if the idea of me
having edges
was something offensive.

And I diminished…
not suddenly,
not enough to alarm anyone…
but in ways no one could measure.


A hesitation here.

A silence there.


A version of me
that became easier to overlook
easier to misplace,
easier to forget.

You asked for everything
and named it love.
I gave it…
until there was nothing left
that felt like mine,
and even that
didn't seem to matter.

Because accountability…
that word never belonged there.
It lingered like something unspoken,
something that would collapse the entire structure
if it were ever acknowledged out loud.

And then it became clear...

That place was never built for truth,
or mutual existence,
or for anything that required multiple people.

It was built
to sustain you.
And anything that didn't fit your blueprint…
you erased.
