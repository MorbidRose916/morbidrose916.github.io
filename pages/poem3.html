Be More Like a Sunflower

Finished: 2025 Published: April 1, 2026

Sunflowers are not soft things…
they are forged in heat.

They rise from dirt that does not care,
push through weight that does not move,
split the ground open with nothing
but a stubborn, living will.

No one teaches them how to find the sun.
They are born knowing
that survival is not enough…
that to live is to reach.

And so they turn.

Through storms that bend their spines,
through days the sky forgets to shine,
they turn…
not because it's easy,
but because something inside them refuses
to face dark for long.

They do not apologize for growing tall.
They do not shrink to be unnoticed.
They stand bright in fields of ordinary,
unafraid of being seen.

And when the season ends,
they do not mourn the light…
they leave behind a thousand seeds,
proof that even in dying,
they gave the world more than they took.

So if you ever doubt your strength,
remember…

you were not made to stay buried.
You were made to break through,
to stand,
to turn,
to endure…

like something
the dark
never learned
how to keep.
