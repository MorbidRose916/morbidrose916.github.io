Time — Now

Finished: 2026 Published: March 29, 2026

Time doesn't pass.
It consumes.

Not loudly…
not all at once…
but with a patience
that guarantees your end.


Tick.
Tick.
Tick.


No warnings.
No permission.

Just movement.

You feel it most
in the quiet…
when distraction fades
and something in you realizes:

nothing is staying.

Not the people.
Not the moments.
Not even the version of you
you swore you'd never lose.

You call it “growing up.”

Time calls it letting go.


You beg for more of it…
like it hasn't already decided
exactly how much you get.

You try to stretch the good,
hold it in place,
live there a little longer…

but it slips.

Every time.

And the bad?

The bad lingers.
Sits heavy.
Makes a home in your chest…
like it has all the time in the world.

Because it does.

You don't.


We say things like
“there's always tomorrow”…

like tomorrow owes us something.

Like time has ever negotiated
with anyone.

Not with you.
Not with me.
Not even with itself.

People disappear inside it.

And what's left…
are questions.

Who would they have become?
What life was waiting for them
that never got the chance to begin?

Time doesn't answer.

It just keeps going.


Tick.
Tick.
Tick.


And somewhere in between those seconds,
you're expected to live.

To build something.
To mean something.
To become
something.

Time isn't just taking from you.
It's shaping you.

Every second…
every loss,
every moment you couldn't hold onto…

it's carving something deeper
than you realize.

You're not the same person
you were a year ago...
or a minute ago.

You're not supposed to be.


So look at the clock.

Not as something chasing you…
but something handing you
what little it can.

Because this second…

this exact, unrepeatable moment in time…

has never existed before.

And it never will again.


Time doesn't change for you.
It never has.

But you…

you can change within it.

So do something with it…
before it decides for you.
