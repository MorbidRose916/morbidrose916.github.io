  • Time — Then
    Finished: 2007 Published: 2008; March 29, 2026

    This is the earliest piece I physically have, and it's the rough draft. I was 17 years old when it was submitted to the "Celebration of Young Poets" contest back in 07', while I attended Susquehannock High School. It was chosen for publication within that years 2007-2008's "Celebration of Young Poets" book.

    Alot of my writing was thrown away upon moving out at 18 years old, this piece is all I got from back then.
