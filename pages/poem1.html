Time — Then

Finished: 2026 Published: March 29, 2026

It doesn't speed up, nor does it stop.
You must live in the moment and cherish.
For you never know what will happen next,
yet you constantly wish to change it.

You want the good to last longer,
the bad to speed through.
Time doesn't even listen to God,
What will make it listen to you?

There are people that pass while time consumes.
Sitting there thinking of what you could have done.
What would his profession have been?
Would have have gotten married or even have a son?

Time is a being but not human.
It is just simply there.
We were given it to do what we need to do,
with so much left to spare.

Look at the time,
for it will be the first and last of its exact.
Time can change only on its own
and we can do nothing but let it pass.
