1. Truth Hurts Once, Lies Hurt Forever

Finished: March 7, 2026 Published: March 22, 2026

I live by a simple principle.
Don't protect me with a lie.
If something will hurt me, let it hurt honestly.

Truth can sting. Sometimes it cuts deep.
It can land hard and force you to face something you didn't want to see.

But it only cuts once.

At least it's honest

It's real.

Lies work differently.
A lie doesn't wound you all at once.
They don't always hurt right away.
Most of the time they arrive wrapped in comfort.
Soft words, reassurance, the illusion that everything is fine.

It lets you believe you are safe.

But that comfort is temporary.

Eventually the lie collapses under its own weight.
And when it does, the damage runs deeper than the truth ever could.

The pain of truth is sharp but brief.
Pain delivered honestly is something you can face.
You can process it, learn from it, heal from it.

The pain of deception lingers.
It leaves questions, broken trust, and scars that take far longer to heal.
The worst damage deception causes isn't the pain it brings.

It's the certainty it takes away.

Because pain eventually fades.
But uncertainty reshapes how you see everything that came before it.

False comfort built on dishonesty leaves something behind.

Not just pain…
But doubt.

Doubt in what was said.
Doubt in what was real.
Doubt in the person who chose deception over respect.
Doubt in how you see yourself.

Doubt in your own sense of reality.


So if I have to choose between the two, the choice is simple.

A hard truth or a comforting lie?

Give me honesty, even when it's difficult.
Let it hurt if it has to.

Truth respects you enough to hurt you.
A lie only protects the one who chose it.
