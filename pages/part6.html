6. Nowhere Left to Hide

Finished: 2024 Published: March 27, 2026

Most people don't process their pain.
They store it.

In entertainment.
In work.
In relationships.
In constant activity just to stay busy.
Sometimes even in healthier things like nature or fitness.

None of those things are bad.

But they become a problem
when they turn into places to store pain
instead of places to process it.

I know this because I did it.

I used them as storage.
I ignored the events that pushed me toward those escapes
just so I could feel happier.


But I wasn't actually happy.


Eventually I realized something.

Even when I thought I was healing…
I was just burying it.

I still acted out.
Many people saw it firsthand.

There were memories I couldn't even think about
without anger or sadness taking over.

So instead of facing them,
I covered them with distractions.
Substances.
Activities.
Anything that kept me from sitting with it.

Then one day it fucking hit me.

I wasn't healing.
I was postponing the process
by refusing to accept what had happened.

The shift didn't start until I allowed myself
to see those memories for what they really were.

Memories.

And they exist for a reason...

Because they hurt.
They hurt like hell.

When I started revisiting them honestly,
I had to ask myself something.

Could I have done things differently?
I had already spent plenty of nights
thinking about that.

But this time I actually dug into it.

I didn't owe explanations to the people involved anymore.
I owed them to myself.

I started recognizing what I
could've done differently back then.

And I began bringing that understanding
into how I live now.

And just because I was fixing parts of myself
didn't mean I could fix anyone else.

People have to reach that place on their own.
And fight through it themselves.

When I started integrating what I had learned,
the change didn't happen overnight.

But eventually…
I noticed something...

A shift.

The anger didn't hit the same.
The memories didn't control the room anymore.

And that's when I realized...

This time...
I hadn't buried the pain.
I didn't store it.
I faced it.

Somewhere in the middle of that process…
something inside me woke up,
that I didn't even know was there.

I didn't start healing when I found my escapes.
I started healing when I ran out of places to hide.
