Brandon...

This space exists for what doesn't get said out loud.

Not everything here is easy to read, and it's not meant to be. It's meant to be mind opening.

My name is Brandon.

I've experienced things I didn't always have the tools to deal with at the time. Some of it stayed buried longer than it should have, and some of it now shapes how I move through life.

Writing became the way I started working through it. Not to soften it or turn it into something it wasn't, but to actually face it and understand it.

That's what this space is for.
