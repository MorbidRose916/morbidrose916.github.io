True North

Direction matters.

This site is a collection of what I build,
what I write, and what I learn along the way.

Nothing here is random.

I started in Army Air Traffic Control, where staying on course wasn't optional.

Now I'm learning to build my own.

True North isn't about a destination... it's a reference point.

This site is where I track the direction... what I'm building, what I'm learning, and whether it lines up.

It keeps me honest with myself.



Outside of all that, I also write.

It's something I go to when I need to slow things down, clear my head, and step away from everything else.

Not everything has to be built. Some things just need to be worked through.
