Brandon...

This space exists for what doesn't get said out loud.

Not everything here is easy to read, and it's not meant to be. It's meant to be felt.

My name is Brandon.

I've experienced things I didn't always have the tools to deal with at the time. Some of it stayed buried longer than it should have, some almost cost me more than I am willing to admit, and some of it now shapes how I move through life.

Writing became the way I started sorting through it. Not to make sense of it right away or pretend it didnt affect me, but to actually face it and understand the hold it had on me.

There will be more. For now, this is where it starts.
